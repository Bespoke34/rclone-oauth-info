Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Purpose and access

This is a private, single-user desktop integration. It accesses the owner's Google Drive only after the owner grants consent, and only to manage the owner's backup files through rclone.

Data handling

The integration does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Backup files are transferred directly between the owner's computers and the owner's Google Drive account through Google's APIs.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration. They are not sent to or stored by this informational website.

Retention and control

Backup copies remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner removes them. The owner can revoke the integration's access at any time from the Google Account security settings.

Website hosting

This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web-request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the owner through the public repository.
